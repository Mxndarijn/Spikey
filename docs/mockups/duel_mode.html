Game modes, Battle and Duel

A mode picker on Home, then two head-to-head modes on one base, with wins leaderboards and bots to get Battle going. Battle (async, first): a long match, first to 6 round wins, taking turns on the same seed, each seeing the other's run as a see-through rival bird. Duel (realtime, later): both fly the same seed at once. Text copy: docs/DUEL_MODE.md.

Plan only · waiting on your answers No sim change, no gameplay-version bump Cosmetic-only monetization kept Modes → Battle → Duel

0. The mode picker

Modes now and soon: Classic (today's endless run), Daily Run, Battle, Duel, and later perhaps Practice or seasonal events. Decided: a mode carousel on Home with Play under it, plus an All modes sheet for when there are more than four or five.

A mode card

  • Icon, name, its rules in one line
  • Badges: your-turn count (Battle), tries left (Daily Run), NEW for a mode's first week
  • Play names the mode: Play, Play Daily Run, Battles, Find a Duel
  • The mode last played stays chosen; Classic is first and the default

Easy to add a mode

A list in game/app/game_modes.gd, one entry per mode: id, title, rule_line, icon, play_label, badge(), open(main), visible(). Home draws the carousel and the sheet from it. A new mode is one entry plus its flow.

Off without an update

The server's ping answer can list modes_off: ["duel"], so a mode can be hidden at once if it misbehaves. Tabs along the top were the alternative: fine for three, crowded at five.

1. Shared match rules

A round is a normal run, GameSim(seed, season), with the server's seed and no revives. These rules hold in Battle and in Duel.

2. Battle (async, built first)

Pick a friend (code or link) or a random player. Each round is a seed. One plays it and submits; the other plays the same seed with that run as the rival bird, and submits. Nobody needs to be online at the same time.

Match length: a long match

Your call: "best of 10 or something". Two ways to do it:

OptionRoundsForAgainst
Decided
First to 6 round wins		6 to 11Ends the moment it is decided, so no dead rounds: a 6-1 is over after 7. At 5-5 one decider settles it.The length varies.
10 rounds, then a tie-break10, or 11 at 5-5Always the same length; a clear "round 7 of 10".Rounds are still played once it is decided (7-1 after 8), so the trailing player has every reason to stop replying.

A drawn round (same score and coins) gives no one a point; after 13 rounds the one with more wins takes it, a level count is a draw. Duel stays best of three: ten live rounds would be 40 minutes in one sitting.

How the turns go

Each turn after the first answers a round and opens the next, so the battle moves on every turn and who opens a round alternates for free. A turn is about 5 minutes of play; at most 12 turns, 6 each.

No turn deadline and no match cap: a battle waits as long as it takes. What keeps that workable:

SettingDecided / recommended
Open at once20 per player (decided), at most 5 random ones waiting for an opponent.
Reminders"Your turn" at once, a nudge after 1 day and after 3 days without a move, then no more. The other player may tap Nudge once a day.
ResignAny time, from the battle's menu, with a confirm. The other player wins. Under rule 12: before round 3 it counts for nobody; from round 3 on a win for them and a loss for you.
Close (no result)When it has been the other player's turn for 7 days: ends the battle for both with no result, not counted, frees your slot. The other sees "Ada closed this battle after 7 days without a move". Against a bot: any time.

The 7 days for Close is not a deadline: nothing happens on its own. It only stops a battle being ended the moment the other player is slow.

Starting a battle

  • Friend: a 6-letter code (no look-alikes) and a link https://flapjaw.mn-platform.tech/battle/KD7WQ3 that opens the app (App Links, Universal Links). 7 days, used once. You play round 1 at once, so your friend finds a run waiting
  • Random: you play round 1 at once and the battle joins a pool. Someone starting a random battle first gets a waiting one (same version, closest hidden rating, never the same pair twice in a row). Nobody waits to play
  • Friends list: later, with stable accounts

One try per run

  • The seed comes only when the run starts (battle_start_run), like a Daily Run try. A started run counts
  • Taps are saved locally as you fly; an app killed mid-run sends what it had, scored as a crash there
  • A run that never arrives (phone gone) scores 0 at your next action in that battle
  • Pause works as in Classic; nobody is waiting live

After an app update

A round is pinned to the gameplay version of its first run. If the second player is on a newer version, the open round is dealt again on the new version for both (new seed, the first player plays it again, no turn lost). Finished rounds stand.

Notifications

Through the server's FCM sender as a new kind in its outbox, on their own Android channel battles (mute battles, keep board alerts), only when push is on. A push opens that battle.

battle_rounds: battle, number, seed, season, first_player, blind, run_a, run_b, winner.

Reminders, no deadlines

A background task in the server, like its checker and push loops (spawn_checker, spawn_push), every few minutes: the 1-day and 3-day nudges go into the push outbox, and random battles waiting past the bot wait get a bot turn queued. Rows are locked with FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED, so a second replica never handles one twice. No CronJob needed.

Anti-cheat

  • Every run checked by the Rust sim like a board run, Godot confirming rejections; only ok runs count or are shown
  • Seed known only after the start, one try
  • end_tick must fit the wall time between start and submit
  • No copied taps: the second player holds the rival's taps, so a modded app could replay them to tie or follow them. Taps matching the rival's within one tick for more than 30 in a row: rejected
  • 20 open, 5 random waiting, 30 creates a day

3. Duel (realtime, later): seeing each other live

Both fly the same seed at the same moment. Round end in Duel: when one crashes at S, the other wins the moment they reach S + 1, or loses by crashing below S.

What is sent

Every 100 ms (6 ticks) each phone sends "I am at tick T, and these are my taps since last time". The "no taps up to T" part is what lets the other phone step the rival safely up to T.

u8 type · varint T · u8 n · n × varint tap delta

Bandwidth

  • Payload: 10 msg/s × about 6 B = 60 B/s
  • With WebSocket, TLS and TCP/IP: about 0.9 KB/s, 7 kbit/s each way
  • A 4-minute round: about 30 KB payload, about 220 KB on the wire
  • Server: about 4 KB/s per live match

Your bird is never delayed

Your sim runs on your phone at 60 Hz exactly as today. Only the rival is drawn a little in the past, at my_tick − D.

When rival data is…What you seeWhat the phone does
within the bufferA smooth rival birdSteps the rival sim exactly with the confirmed taps. D drifts slowly by playing the rival at 0.95× or 1.05× for a moment, never a jump.
late, up to 0.5 sStill smooth, maybe a small easeSteps a copy (SimCopy) guessing no tap. When the real taps come, the copy is dropped, the rival sim steps on exactly from its last confirmed state, and the drawn bird eases to the true spot over 6 frames. A guessed crash is never shown.
0.5 to 5 s lateRival fades to 25% with a connection iconGrows D so it plays smoothly again once data comes back.
over 5 s"Mira is reconnecting"Your round goes on; the server's rejoin rules decide (section 3).

Drawing the rival without touching your sim

services/duel_link.gd

The WebSocketPeer, encodes and decodes tick batches. Uses only sim/ and meta/.

meta/match_rules.gd

Best of three, the S + 1 clinch, ties, time limit. Pure rules like DailyRunRules; the server has the same rules in Rust, held together by shared fixtures.

game/match/rival_driver.gd

Owns a second GameSim, steps it only from DuelLink's taps (as the replay driver does for Watch), holds the jitter buffer and the guess-and-correct copy.

game/match/rival_view.gd

A BirdView in the rival's look at 45%, name tag, WILD tag, crash puff. Draws no room and no hazards; your RoomView stays yours. No sounds but a soft crash.

sim/ does not change; it already runs many sims side by side (bots, SurvivalSearch). Your RunController and its sim are untouched; game/ writes to neither sim. test_layers.gd holds as it is.

Duel network and server

Transport

  • wss://flapjaw.mn-platform.tech/v1/duel/ws, axum WebSockets; Traefik passes them through
  • Sign-in: the HTTP API's access token in the first message
  • Godot's built-in WebSocketPeer, Android and iOS
  • One tokio task per match holds both players' Rust sims
  • One replica for now; more later need sticky routing by match id
  • Each round's seeds, taps and result go to Postgres (duel_matches, duel_rounds)

Matchmaking

  • Invite code or link : the same 6-letter codes as Battle, with no look-alikes, https://flapjaw.mn-platform.tech/duel/ABC123 opens the app; 10 minutes
  • Random queue : same gameplay version, then a hidden Glicko-2 Duel rating whose range widens every 5 s. Nobody after 30 s: offer a Battle instead
  • Friends (later): needs stable accounts, Apple and Google sign-in from the phone-transfer plan

Rejoin and forfeit

  • In a round your bird keeps flying; taps within 5 s of the server's clock count. Past 5 s: a crash at the last confirmed tick
  • Back within 30 s: you play the next round
  • Between rounds: 30 s to return, else the match is forfeited
  • Leave = forfeit; 3 forfeits a day = 10 minutes out of the queue
  • Server restart: matches in play end with no result; deploys wait for live matches (10 minutes at most)

Anti-cheat: the server judges

  • The server steps both sims from the taps; phones never report the result
  • Clock check: ticks must follow the server clock since the countdown within 5 s. Stops playing a hard room in slow motion, the one cheat realtime adds
  • Taps ascending, one a tick at most, 20000 a round at most
  • A disagreement goes to the headless Godot check, as today
  • Perfect bots: plausibility checks into a review queue; no money at stake

5. Wins leaderboards

Who wins the most Battles and Duels, on the leaderboard screen next to the score boards. Today's screen has one tab bar (Today, Daily Run, All-time), a podium, pill rows and your own row pinned at the bottom; five or six tabs in one bar don't fit a small phone, so Wins gets its own switch.

Ranking

  • By counted wins; ties: higher win rate, then who got there first
  • Win rate shown on every row, "–" below 10 counted matches (weekly: 3). Not used for ranking; a win-rate board can come later
  • This week (Mon–Sun, Amsterdam, the boards' day zone) and All time. A battle counts in the week it ends
  • Top 100 plus your own place, like the score boards. No prizes for now

Server

  • Table match_results: match, mode, winner, loser, draw, ended_by, counted, reason, finished_at, week; written in the transaction that ends a match
  • get_wins_board(mode, period): top 100, your place, players, reset time
  • get_match_record(player, mode) for the record sheet
  • Client: LeaderboardService.WINS_BOARDS beside BOARDS, same podium and rows
  • counted plus reason are stored, so rules can be tuned and boards recomputed

Stopping win farming

The easy farm: two own accounts, or a willing friend, playing each other with one losing on purpose. All of this runs on the server.

Recommended

Random counts, friends capped

  • Random matches count in full: pairing by rating, never the same pair twice in a row, so own accounts rarely meet
  • Friend matches: 1 counted win per opponent a week, 3 all time; your own record still shows them all

No tanking

A match counts only if the loser's runs average at least 5 points: real runs, not a crash on the first wall.

No alt accounts

The loser's account must be 3 days old with 20 checked runs. Never counted when both used the same device key, or the same IP during the match (IPs kept hashed for 7 days, as the transfer limits already do). Sudden jumps go to the review queue.

6. Bots kickstart Battle

With few players a random battle waits a long time for a person. Your idea: let the beast-mode bots play Battle turns from the server first, then match active players to each other automatically, and phase the bots out once there are enough players.

The bot

  • The FastSim-RS human-model bot: BlindPlanner plus a measured human motor model (docs/HUMAN_BOT.md)
  • Screen-only sight: it plans on a copy of the sim with the hidden values (next wall, spawns, hidden exits) replaced; the FastSim sight check keeps it honest
  • Human limits: the game's 100 ms tap gap, the 8-tick thumb gap, tap timing error, a reaction time to new spikes and tells
  • Plays the seed on its own and never reads your run, so it can't copy

Skill matched to you

  • Five anchors: beginner 30, normal 60, good 120, expert 250, beast 500+ median points; tap spread and reaction time interpolated between them
  • Level picked from your hidden Battle rating so it wins about half the rounds
  • Moves after every battle: a bot that won 6-1 plays a notch weaker next time
  • Answers 2 to 30 minutes after your turn, never hours

Never misleading

  • A BOT pill beside its name everywhere: "Flapbot · Good"
  • New battle says: "Random rival: a player, or a bot when no one is free"
  • Free starter looks only
  • Bot matches never count on the wins boards; they show in your record as "vs bot". Coins still go to gold. Your hidden rating moves at half weight

Three phases

a · bots kickstart

Bots fill random battles

Every random battle no person takes within 10 minutes gets a bot. A player with no battle open sees "Start a battle" on Home; the bot answers within minutes.

b · auto-matchmaking

Players who play get battles

After a Classic run or an app open, a player with Auto battles on (Settings, on by default) and fewer than 3 auto battles open gets a waiting random battle of a person near their rating: "New battle from Jun, your turn". People first; a bot only when no person is found in the wait time.

c · bots out

The bot wait grows

10 minutes, then 2 hours, then 12 hours, then bots stop filling random battles. Open bot battles finish normally. "Play a bot" can stay in New battle as practice. The wait is bot_wait_s in ping, set by an admin op, so it needs no update.

The worker

A small Rust binary on the fastsim crate (human module and sim), polling a bot_turns queue (FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED) and submitting through the same database functions as a player. One replica, CPU limit 500m, memory limit 128 MB (beam copies of the sim are about 1 KB each).

CPU per turn

The BlindPlanner runs about 14,000 ticks/s on 4 threads of a Ryzen 9 9950X, so about 3,500 a core; a VM core is taken as half, about 1,700 ticks/s.

  • Typical round (score about 30, 2,700 ticks): about 1.6 s
  • A 4-minute round (14,400 ticks): about 8.5 s
  • A bot turn (two runs): 3 to 17 s; expert and beast use the fine search (about 3.6x) and sit at the top
  • At 500m: about 2,500 to 14,000 bot turns a day, far more than phase a needs

Decided

  • Mode picker: the carousel on Home plus the All modes sheet.
  • Battle is first to 6 round wins.
  • The player who starts the battle opens round 1, then the opener alternates; round 11 (at 5-5) is blind for both. The page assumed this already.
  • No battle timers: no turn deadline, no match cap. Instead nudges, Resign, and Close (no result) after 7 days waiting.
  • At most 20 open battles per player.
  • The rival is drawn in their own skin, slightly see-through; no new colour token.
  • As recommended: Duel round end at S + 1; coins to gold only, no diamonds; a hidden rating; the wins boards as shown; random matches count in full, friend wins at most 1 per opponent a week; a resign before round 3 counts for nobody, later as a win and a loss.
