Flapjaw · design proposal · not built

Boss Rooms

At 100, 200, 300 and on, the portal leads into a boss room. Every wall bounce is a hit on the boss. Twenty bounces break it, in three phases, and every attack shows itself one second before it can hurt you.

World
Start

The exit at 100 is a boss portal

The exit is planned on the 99th point like any other and shows as a ghost with a BOSS tag. Out of it the room holds 1.5 s instead of 0.5 s (taps ignored) while the boss comes in and its name shows. The first tell starts no sooner than 1 s after the bird can move.

Length

20 bounces, three phases, about 23 s

A crossing is 70 ticks (1.17 s), so 20 bounces take 23 s. The boss bar has 20 pips and every bounce takes one. Phases change at 7 and 14 bounces. Between phases the boss staggers for one crossing with no new attack: that stagger is the tell for the phase change. There is no exit at 110.

End

At 120 the boss breaks

Its hazards stop and fade, so the last crossing is free. The exit opens on the wall ahead (planned and ghosted on the 119th point). Reward: a coin burst into the counter (15 at 100, 20 at 200, 25 from 300) and a first-clear trophy in Rooms. Cosmetic money only, and the score stays bounces.

Harder later

Tier = score ÷ 100

Tier 1: one attack at a time. Tier 2 (200): phase 3 runs two attacks together under the Double Trouble combo rules, shorter rests, one more wall spike. Tier 3 (300 and on): each boss's rage variant, two more wall spikes. What never changes: tells of 1 s or more, a free band of 120 px or more, 20 bounces.

Fork

The fork moves to the boss exit

If Calm/Wild stays, 100 and 200 get one boss portal and the boss's exit at 120 and 220 is the fork (Wild still pays its 3 coins). If fork-choice drops the fork, the boss exit is one plain portal. A boss on the Wild route only would let a 1v1 player skip it, so that is not proposed.

Death and revive

The room restarts as entered

A death in a boss room is a normal death. A revive works as in revive-reset: score back to 100, the boss at a full bar, coins as at entry, the same attack clock. No phase checkpoints: a checkpoint makes a revive worth more in a boss room and puts two 1v1 players on different clocks.

Fair way through

Every safe line is a height

The bird can't choose where it is across the room, only how high it is. So every attack leaves a band of heights open, at least 120 px (bird 28, hop 60, margin), and its tell says which heights. Each boss and tier gets a FastSim check: simple players must live through every phase, like ArrivalCheck.

Same seed

One clock for everyone

Speed across is fixed, so every player bounces on the same ticks and the boss's phases and tells land on the same ticks. Lanes and columns come from the room seed. Attacks aimed at the bird read its height when the tell starts, like Hunter Spike: that is the player's own choice. Which boss comes is a spine draw from the bosses not met yet in this run.

Where they sit in a run

Scores 80 to 230 with the fork kept (option A). Each block is one room; a boss room is two rooms long.

Open questions

  1. Which bosses first? Suggestion: The Watcher and The Crusher. They reuse Beam Gates, Hunter Spike, Piston and Lava Rise almost as they are. The Gatekeeper and The Shade next: they reuse Shutter Gates and Echo but need their own boss rules. Saw King last.
  2. Length: 20 bounces (proposed, three phases, two rooms long) or 10 (keeps the 10-point cadence, about 12 s, two phases)?
  3. Reward: coins 15 / 20 / 25 plus a trophy, or also a boss-only cosmetic, for example a crown hat for breaking all four?
  4. Fork: boss exit becomes the Calm/Wild fork (option A), or one plain exit if fork-choice drops forks. With option A, forks sit at 120 and 150, close together; should 150 go?
  5. Boss order: seeded draw from the bosses not met in the run (proposed), or a fixed order so 100 is always the same, easiest boss?
  6. Revive: full restart at 100 (proposed) or a checkpoint at the phase start?
  7. Wall spikes: 3 per wall at tier 1 instead of the usual 8 at that score, since the boss is the threat. OK?
