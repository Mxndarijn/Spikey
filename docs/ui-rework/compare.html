Flapjaw UI rework

Every screen of the app before and after the rework, shot in the game at phone size (648 × 1404, a 9:19.5 phone). The headline change: Home's mode carousel and the All modes sheet are replaced by mode tabs, Classic | Daily | Battle | Duel, with badges, and Play follows the chosen tab.

12 of 29 views changed. Screens that already followed the Flapjaw Design Kit are marked Same and shown smaller. UI only: no change to the game, the hitbox or the shop.

Made with tools/capture_ui.ps1 on branch ui-rework. Fake battles, boards and a Daily Run fill the screens; the app was offline, so the Wins board shows its real offline toast.

Home

Changed

The mode carousel and its All modes link are gone. A tab bar, Classic | Daily | Battle | Duel, sits under the stats line with the chosen mode's rules under it. Gold badges show the Daily Run's tries left and the battles waiting for your turn. The offline "No connection" toast no longer sits on the wordmark: Home's background battle check stays quiet.

Choosing a mode

Changed

Before, the All modes sheet was the only way to see every mode at once. Now all four are always on screen: one tap on a tab chooses it and Play in the dock follows it (Play, Daily, Battles, Duel).

Battle tab

Changed

With Battle chosen, the line under the tabs counts your turns ("2 battles wait for your turn") and Play opens Battles. An app too old for Battle and Duel shows an UPDATE badge there, and Play opens the store.
