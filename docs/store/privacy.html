Flapjaw Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}. Applies to the Flapjaw app for Android and iOS and its game server at flapjaw.mn-platform.tech.

In short. Flapjaw has no sign-up: no email, no password, no real name. Your phone gets a random key that is your account. Our server keeps your player name, your runs (score and replay), your Battles and Duels, your game save and, if you allow notifications, a push token. We use no analytics. The only ads are the rewarded ads you choose to watch for a revive, served by Google AdMob. We don't sell your data. Mail {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} to have everything deleted.

What our server stores

DataWhat it isWhy
Account A random player id, and a key your phone makes the first time you play. We keep only a one-way hash of that key and of your sign-in tokens, plus when they were made. So your phone can sign in to the boards, Battles and Duels without a password.
Player name and look The name you pick (3 to 14 letters, digits, dots or underscores) and the flyer and cosmetics you use. Names pass a word filter. Shown to other players on the leaderboards, in replays, Battles and Duels.
Runs and replays For each run you send: score, coins, the day, the map seed, the game version, the moments you tapped (that is the replay), revives used, the cosmetics you wore and whether the run passed our check. Leaderboards, the Daily Run, replays other players can watch, and replaying each run on our server to keep cheated scores off the boards.
Battles and Duels Who played whom, each round's map, runs, scores and winner, your wins and losses, and for bots their level. In a Duel your taps are passed to your rival live while you play. Running the match and the Wins boards.
Game save Your settings, coins, diamonds and their history (what you won, spent or bought), cosmetics you own, and Daily Run tries and prizes. So a new phone or a reinstall gets your progress back, and so prizes and purchases are counted once.
Push token Only if notifications are on: the token Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) or Apple gives your phone, the platform, your time zone offset and the on/off switch, plus the alerts queued for you. Telling you it's your turn in a Battle or that someone passed you on a board, at a sensible hour. Turn it off with Board alerts in the game's settings, or in your phone's settings.
Account transfer When you move your account to another phone: a hash of the code, when it was made and used, and the IP address of the phone that used it. Moving your account safely, and spotting someone trying to take over an account.

We don't ask for or store your email, phone number, real name, contacts, photos or precise location. Purchases (once they open) are handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details, only which product was bought.

What stays on your phone

Your game save, settings and recent runs are also kept on your phone. Deleting the app removes them from the phone (your server copy stays until you ask us to delete it).

Other companies involved

Google AdMob (ads)

The only ads in Flapjaw are rewarded ads you choose to watch to revive. They come from Google AdMob. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are first asked for consent through Google's consent form; you can change your choice later in the game under Settings → About → Ad choices. Without consent no ads are loaded and you keep the free revives. When ads are on, Google's SDK collects data such as your device's advertising id, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with the ad, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. We set ads to suit a general audience (no content above "parental guidance"). See how Google uses data from apps. On iPhone, Apple asks whether the app may track you; saying no still lets you watch ads.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising id in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service

Deliver our notifications to your phone. They handle the push token described above. We use no other Firebase services: Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are switched off.

Hetzner (hosting)

Our server and its backups run at Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany. Your data stays in the European Union.

Google Play and Apple App Store

Distribute the app and handle purchases under their own privacy policies.

What other players see

Your player name, your look, your scores and your replays are public to other Flapjaw players on the leaderboards, and your rival sees your run in a Battle or Duel. Pick a name that isn't your real name.

Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Providing the game you asked for (contract): account, name, runs, Battles, Duels, game save, prizes.
  • Legitimate interest: checking runs for cheating, rate limits and the transfer IP to stop abuse, backups.
  • Consent: notifications, and ads where the law requires consent. You can withdraw it at any time in the game's or your phone's settings.

How long we keep it

  • Account, name, runs, Battles, Duels and game save: as long as your account exists. Delete it any time (below).
  • Push token: until you turn notifications off, Google or Apple tell us it no longer works, or your account is deleted. Queued alerts older than 30 minutes are never sent.
  • Transfer codes expire quickly; the transfer record (with the IP address) stays with your account.
  • Backups of the database are kept for 14 days, so deleted data is gone from backups within 14 days.

Deleting your account and data

Mail {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the subject "Delete my Flapjaw account" and your player name. To prove the account is yours, open Settings → Move to a new phone in the game, make a transfer code and put it in the mail (the code lets nobody else in once we've deleted the account). We delete your account and everything linked to it (name, runs, replays, Battles, Duels, save, push token, transfer records) within 30 days and mail you when it's done. Your name then disappears from the boards. Results your rivals keep (for example "won against a deleted player") stay without your name.

You can also ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to stop a use based on legitimate interest, the same way.

Children

Flapjaw is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used Flapjaw, mail us and we'll delete the account.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and object to processing. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or the authority where you live.

Changes

When the game starts using data in a new way we update this page and the date at the top, and say so in the release notes.
